Action row at an item's end

Each case measures an action row against the copy above it and against its neighbor's row. In items that stretch to their grid row, the modifier puts each row at its item's end, so neighboring rows share a bottom edge, and the tallest item keeps its gap after its copy. Without the modifier, or where the row has no free space, a row stays after its copy. When one row wraps to more lines than its neighbor's, the bottoms still meet and the tops do not.

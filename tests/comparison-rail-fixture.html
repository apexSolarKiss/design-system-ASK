Comparison rails

Each case is measured once the fonts are ready. A rail never begins before the content edge of the header that governs its cell: the column header for a cell in one column, the group header for a cell that spans the group. Outside a value field it begins on that edge, and the cell's text keeps the lane's 12px after the rail; a value field's rail sits 12px before the field. Assessed and unassessed cells share one origin, figures keep the end, the key keeps its own geometry, and a table that scrolls carries its rails with its text.

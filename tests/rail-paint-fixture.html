Rail paint

Each case is measured once the fonts are ready. A rail in one governed color takes the same paint whichever meaning draws it: a review range's scope rail, a keyed state or evidence range's and a comparison's assessment rail in green or red are painted alike, and caution's yellow is the same plain stroke beside a summary, in a value field, in a key, in a dark subtree and beside a structural state range. Each paint is keyed by its color, and the meaning-named paints are aliases that read it. When the shared paint changes, every rail bound to it follows; the bordered label and the passage rail keep their own treatments.
